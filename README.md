# Kebabessy

An application that guides you to the nearest Kebab.

## Introduction - Why Kebabessy?

The Kebab is no mere meal, it is an all-powerful being, spinning eternally, watching over the city with silent, savory wisdom. Its presence bends hunger and fate alike, drawing you closer with every step until you stand before it, chosen.

With that in mind, it's important to always know where the nearest Kebab is, so it can restore you with rejuvenating strength.
That's why I decided to create **Kebabessy**, to answer that need in a fast and simple way.
